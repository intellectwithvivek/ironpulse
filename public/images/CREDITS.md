# Photo credits

All photographs in this directory ship with the template so the site renders on
any network, with no third-party image host and no allow-list. They are
right-sized to how they actually render on the page rather than to their source
resolution, and converted to AVIF where the source offered it.

Replacing them is the expected thing to do — point the paths in
[`data/classes.ts`](../../data/classes.ts),
[`data/trainers.ts`](../../data/trainers.ts) and
[`data/gym.ts`](../../data/gym.ts) at your own files.

## A note on the people in these photographs

The coaches, members and before/after pairs on this site are **fictional**. The
names, quotes, specialities and results in `data/` are written for a template;
they are not claims about the people pictured, and nobody shown here has endorsed
a gym that does not exist.

If you ship this template as a real business site, replace every photograph of a
person with one you have the right to use and consent for.
